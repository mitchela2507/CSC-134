# The Sandwich

## Assumptions
- Bag of sliced loaf bread, the peanut butter jar, a knife on the countertop, and a plate

## Steps
- Open bag of sliced bread and then get out 2 slices of bread from the bread bag and place them on the plate. Close bag back up and set it aside.
- Get the jar of peanut butter, then open and remove lid from jar. Set lid aside and then grab the knife to put inside of the peanut butter.
- Remove kinfe from the peanut butter jar and put down the jar.
- Grab bread from plate then start to spread the peanut butter that is on the knife onto the bread.
- When finsih spreading the peanut butter put down the knife and then put the covered bread on top of the other slice of bread that's on the plate.
- Then pick up the knife and cut the completed sandwich into 2 triangles. Then put the knife back down on the countertop 

## Done When
- The sandwich is completely made and cut into 2 triangles on the plate.
